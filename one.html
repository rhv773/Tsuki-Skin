What is the Initial Skin Assessment?

The Initial Skin Assessment is a personalized questionnaire designed to help us understand your skin type, concerns, and needs. Based on your answers, you can receive personalized recommendations for your skincare journey.

Do I need to create an account to take the assessment?

Yes. Creating an account allows us to securely save your assessment and skin profile, so you can access them again whenever you need.

Can I book an appointment with a doctor?

Yes. You can explore our doctors, view their profiles, and book an appointment with the doctor who best suits your needs.

Can I change my skin profile information?

Yes. You can update your personal and skin profile information through your account whenever needed.

Can I download my skin profile?

Yes. Your personalized skin profile can be downloaded as a PDF for easy access and future reference.

When are laboratory tests recommended?

Laboratory tests may be recommended when additional information could help provide a better understanding of your skin or overall needs. You can then access our laboratory partner’s exclusive discount.

How does the VIP Membership work?

Our VIP Membership gives you access to exclusive benefits, including priority booking, discounts on doctor consultations and products, birthday benefits, exclusive events, and free delivery.

Do you offer delivery for products?

Yes. We offer product delivery, with free delivery included for VIP members.

Can I get help if I have more questions?

Of course. If you need further assistance, you can contact our team through the Contact Us section, and we will be happy to help.
