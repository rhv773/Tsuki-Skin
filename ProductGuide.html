⚠️

Using Too Many Active Ingredients

Why it can be harmful:

It can irritate your skin, damage your skin barrier and cause dryness, redness or breakouts.

How to avoid it:

Start with one or two actives, use them on different days if needed, and always listen to your skin.

5. Product Compatibility

Some products work well together, while others can cause irritation or dryness. Here's what to keep in mind:

✔

Using a cleanser + moisturizer + sunscreen is safe and effective for most skin types.

⚠️

Avoid using multiple strong exfoliants in the same routine.

⚠️

Be careful when combining acne treatments (e.g., retinol, benzoyl peroxide, salicylic acid).

✔

Some products need special instructions from your doctor.
